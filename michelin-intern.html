Michelin, Pune · Dec 2021 – May 2022

Detecting Axle Misalignment Before It's a Problem

Multi-axle commercial trucks lose fuel efficiency and tire life to axle misalignment that's often invisible until it's already causing damage. I built a physics-based model using telematics and CAN data to catch it early — detecting misalignment down to 0.06 degrees, with 94% accuracy and 96% precision across a range of real-world operating and environmental conditions.

This work fed directly into diagnostic workflows for fleet operators, giving them a way to flag misalignment issues before they showed up as measurable tire wear or fuel economy loss.
