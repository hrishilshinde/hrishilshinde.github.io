Optimization of Motor Losses in Induction Motor-Based EV Drives

Final Year Thesis · IIT Madras · 2022 – 2023

Induction motors remain a popular choice for EV drivetrains due to their robustness and cost, but they suffer from higher losses than permanent magnet alternatives — especially under varying drive cycle conditions. My thesis focused on closing that efficiency gap through optimization rather than hardware changes.

I implemented constrained optimization models targeting system-level loss minimization, then simulated dynamic system models to evaluate efficiency gains under realistic operating and drive cycle conditions rather than idealized steady-state assumptions. The result was a nearly 40% reduction in modelled system losses across both transient and steady-state operation.

Conference Presentation

This work was presented as "Drive Cycle-Based Loss Minimization Strategies for Induction Motor Drives in Electrified Vehicles" at the IEEE International Transportation Electrification Conference (ITEC), Chennai, India, 2023.
